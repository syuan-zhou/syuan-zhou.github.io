About Me

Dr. Shiyuan Zhou
Assistant Professor, Chair of Department of BigData
School of Information Engineering
Jiaxing Nanhu University

Email: shiyuan.zhou [at] zjxu.edu.cn
Github: https://github.com/syuan-zhou/

Dr. Shiyuan Zhou is currently a Lecturer at the School of Information Engineering, Jiaxing Nanhu University (JXNHU). He received his Ph.D. degree in Management Science and Engineering (E-commerce) from the School of Information Management and Engineering, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE), in 2021, under the supervision of Prof. Yinglin Wang. Prior to his current position, he was a Visiting Scholar at the City University of Macau. His research interests primarily focus on Service Computing, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Intelligent Computing. Dr. Zhou has served as an editorial board member, program committee member, and reviewer for various academic journals and conferences. He is a dedicated member of the China Computer Federation (CCF) and the Technical Committee on Network Science and Engineering, Chinese Institute of Command and Control.
